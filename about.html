Researcher Profile

I am a Computer Science student and researcher focused on the theoretical foundations and practical applications of artificial intelligence. My work spans from training large-scale neural networks to deploying efficient models in resource-constrained environments.

Currently pursuing my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering at University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh, I have developed expertise in deep learning architectures, natural language processing, and computer vision systems. My research has resulted in three peer-reviewed publications in international conferences, addressing critical challenges in healthcare AI, assistive technology, and smart city infrastructure.

My current research interests center on Large Language Models and the path toward Artificial General Intelligence. I am particularly interested in understanding emergent capabilities in foundation models, developing efficient fine-tuning methodologies, and exploring alignment techniques that ensure robust and beneficial AI systems.
